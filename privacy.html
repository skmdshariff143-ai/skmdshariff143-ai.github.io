3. Third-Party Service Providers

The following external service providers participate in delivering assets and form routing:

  • Hosting & Edge Delivery (Vercel Inc.): Deployed on Vercel's global edge network. Vercel edge servers process HTTP requests ephemerally and automatically generate server access logs (including visitor IP addresses, timestamps, HTTP method/path, and User-Agent) for DDoS mitigation, traffic routing, and operational security. Per Vercel's official documentation (Vercel Runtime Logs), runtime edge access logs are retained ephemerally: 1 hour on the Hobby tier and 1 day on the Pro tier.
  • Form Routing & Storage (Formspree Inc.): Submissions through the contact form are transmitted directly via HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) to Formspree (endpoint: https://formspree.io/f/mvzjyllq). Formspree processes submissions as a service provider, storing submission data encrypted at rest (AES-256 block-level storage encryption) on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure located in the United States, and dispatches email notifications to the site owner. Per Formspree's official documentation (Formspree Plans and Privacy Policy), submission archive retention is plan-dependent ([my Formspree plan is: free / paid]): on the Free plan, the Submission Archive is limited to 30 Days; higher plans (Personal, Professional, Business) provide higher limits or custom retention. Spam mitigation uses Formshield machine learning; custom domain restrictions and CAPTCHA challenge validation must be checked and configured in the site owner's Formspree dashboard.
  • Typography (Google Fonts — Google LLC): The website requests Google Font stylesheets (fonts.googleapis.com) and font binary files (fonts.gstatic.com). When downloading these files, your IP address and User-Agent are transmitted directly to Google's servers.
  • Iconography & Vendor Assets (cdnjs — Cloudflare Inc.): Font Awesome icon stylesheets and assets are retrieved from Cloudflare's cdnjs network (cdnjs.cloudflare.com), which processes the visitor's IP address and browser headers to fulfill static asset delivery.

4. Cookie & Local Storage Disclosure

This site sets no cookies. No HTTP cookies (session, persistent, or third-party) are placed on your browser by this origin.

The site uses browser client-side web storage exclusively for device-local functional behavior:

  • localStorage key portfolio_reduced_motion: stores boolean preference ('true' or 'false') when toggling motion settings, persisting whether Three.js 3D animations are enabled or reduced across visits.
  • sessionStorage key portfolio_last_submit_time: stores the unix epoch millisecond timestamp of your latest message submission during the active session. This powers a cosmetic 30-second client-side cooldown timer to prevent accidental double-clicks. This control is cosmetic, operates strictly on your client device, and does not replace server-side spam or rate-limiting safeguards.

These values reside purely on your device, are never transmitted to external servers, and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.
