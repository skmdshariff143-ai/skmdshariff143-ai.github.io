3. Third-Party Service Providers

The following third-party infrastructure providers participate in rendering and supporting this website:

  • Hosting & Edge Delivery: The site is deployed on Vercel Inc. Edge servers process HTTP requests ephemerally and retain aggregated edge access logs for security operations (typically up to 7 days).
  • Contact Form Processing: When you submit the contact form, your input is securely transmitted via HTTPS directly to Formspree Inc. (formspree.io/f/mnnjgkve), which forwards the notification to my institutional email. Messages remain in Formspree until reviewed or deleted.
  • External Content Delivery Networks: Static web fonts are delivered via Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) and select third-party icon libraries via CDNJS (cdnjs.cloudflare.com). These requests transmit your IP address to those CDNs solely to deliver the requested asset files.

4. Cookies & Client-Side Storage

This website uses zero third-party advertising cookies, zero trackers, and zero analytics scripts. The only client-side storage utilized is browser localStorage, which stores:

  • portfolio-theme: remembers whether you selected Dark or Light theme.
  • portfolio-motion-preference: remembers whether you paused the Three.js 3D background animation.

These values reside purely on your device, are never transmitted to external servers, and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.
