3. Third-Party Service Providers

The following external service providers participate in delivering assets and form routing:

  • Hosting & Edge Delivery (Vercel Inc.): Deployed on Vercel's global edge network. Vercel edge servers process HTTP requests ephemerally and automatically maintain server access logs (including visitor IP addresses, timestamps, and HTTP headers) for security monitoring and operational diagnosis. Per Vercel's platform documentation, edge access logs are retained for up to 7 days.
  • Form Routing & Storage (Formspree Inc.): Submissions through the contact form are transmitted directly via HTTPS to Formspree (endpoint: https://formspree.io/f/mvzjyllq). Formspree processes submissions as a data processor, encrypts data in transit and at rest in the United States, and dispatches email notifications to the site owner. According to published documentation (Formspree Privacy Policy), submissions remain stored in Formspree's database until manually deleted or archived by the account owner, or until the form/account is terminated.
  • Typography (Google Fonts — Google LLC): The website requests Google Font stylesheets (fonts.googleapis.com) and font binary files (fonts.gstatic.com). When downloading these files, your IP address and User-Agent are transmitted directly to Google's servers.
  • Iconography & Vendor Assets (cdnjs — Cloudflare Inc.): Font Awesome icon stylesheets and assets are retrieved from Cloudflare's cdnjs network (cdnjs.cloudflare.com), which processes the visitor's IP address and browser headers to fulfill static asset delivery.

4. Cookie & Local Storage Disclosure

This site sets no cookies. No HTTP cookies (session, persistent, or third-party) are placed on your browser by this origin.

The site uses browser localStorage purely to remember client-side display preferences:

  • portfolio-theme: remembers light or dark theme toggle.
  • portfolio-motion-preference: remembers whether Three.js 3D canvas animation is enabled or reduced.

These values reside purely on your device, are never transmitted to external servers, and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.
